02 About Us

A Local Iron & Steel Store You Can Rely On

Mithlesh Iron Store is a local supplier of iron, steel and hardware materials, serving homeowners, contractors, builders and fabricators. We focus on maintaining consistent stock, clear pricing and dependable service so your project timelines stay on track.

From TMT bars for foundations to angles and channels for fabrication work, we aim to be a single, trustworthy stop for the materials your project needs.
